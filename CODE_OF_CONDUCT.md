# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people who improve the Skill through code, documentation, testing, translation, failure reports, or careful disagreement.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, respectful, and open to correction.
- Critique claims, methods, and artifacts rather than people.
- Disclose uncertainty and preserve negative or null evaluation results.
- Protect the privacy of users, candidates, employers, and research participants.
- Make room for newcomers and explain specialist terms when they matter.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or targeted personal attacks.
- Publishing resumes, contact details, confidential records, private prompts, or identifying research data without permission.
- Fabricating evaluation results, citations, candidate achievements, or community endorsements.
- Using project spaces to spam employers, candidates, or contributors.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately through GitHub Security Advisories when public disclosure could harm someone. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
